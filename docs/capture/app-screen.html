Comparison of Low-Light Enhancement Methods for Night-Time Pedestrian Detection

예시_야간보행자검출_원고.pdf10쪽참고문헌 8건 검토 2026-11-04 10:50분석 18.4초 Keywords: Low-Light Enhancement, Pedestrian Detection, Night Vision, Image Fusion
초록

AbstractThis study compares low-light image enhancement methods for night-time pedestrian detection. We evaluate four enhancement techniques with two detectors on a newly collected urban night dataset and show that enhancement improves recall in dark scenes.
